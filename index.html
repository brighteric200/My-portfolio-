Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

What do you build?
Business websites, web apps with sign-in and booking, e-commerce storefronts, and data dashboards. If it runs in a browser, I can probably build it.
How long does a website take?
A focused business site usually takes one to two weeks once I have your content. Web apps with accounts or databases take longer, and I give you a timeline after our first call.
How much does it cost?
It depends on scope: pages, features and how much content is ready. Pick a budget range in the project form below and I will reply with a clear quote and what it includes.
Will it work on phones?
Yes. Everything I build is mobile-first and tested on small screens, because most of your visitors will be on a phone.
Do you help with hosting and domains?
Yes. I deploy to fast hosts such as Netlify or GitHub Pages, connect your domain, and show you how to make simple updates yourself.
Can you analyse my business data?
Yes. Send me a spreadsheet or export, and I will clean it, find the trends and build a dashboard like the one above, or a short written report.
How do we get started?
Fill in the project form or message me on WhatsApp. Tell me what you need and when, and I will reply with next steps.
